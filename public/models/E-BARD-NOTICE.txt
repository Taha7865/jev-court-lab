The author's BODD_yolov8n_0001.pt weights were converted to ONNX (opset 17,
704×704 float32 input) for browser inference. No retraining was performed.
Classes: basketball, hoop, player, referee.
The original model card describes limitations for small objects and non-NBA footage.
Built on the Ultralytics YOLOv8 architecture (AGPL-3.0):
